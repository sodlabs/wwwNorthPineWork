Services

Three ways to work with us.

Each service can stand alone or combine into a longer engagement. We scope every project before quoting it, no packaged tiers that don't fit your system.

Infrastructure & reliability

Architecture reviews, deployment pipelines, observability, and incident readiness. We look at the system the way it behaves at 3 a.m. during an outage, not the way it looks in a diagram.

  • Dependency and failure-mode mapping
  • Deployment & rollback pipeline design
  • Observability and alerting review
  • Capacity and scaling assessment

Security review

A prioritized review of the paths an attacker would actually take, not a generic checklist. We focus on trust boundaries, session handling, and access control.

  • Threat modeling for your specific system
  • Authentication & session review
  • Access control and privilege boundaries
  • Remediation plan, ranked by risk

Backend engineering

APIs, data models, and services designed around how your product actually grows, not a generic best-practice template.

  • API and data model design
  • Migration from legacy systems
  • Performance and query optimization
  • Ongoing implementation support

Engagement models

How the work is structured

Fixed-scope assessment

A two-to-four week engagement that ends in a written report: findings, priorities and a recommendation, including "don't change this yet" where that's the honest answer.

Embedded engineering

Our team works alongside yours for a defined period, building against the plan and documenting decisions as we go, so the knowledge stays with your team after we leave.
