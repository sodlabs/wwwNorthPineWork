Industries

Domains where reliability isn't optional.

We take on a small number of long-term clients rather than a large number of short ones. Most of our work has been in these areas.

Finance & payments

Systems where a quiet failure means a reconciliation problem, not just downtime. We work closely with compliance and audit requirements rather than around them.

Logistics & operations

Infrastructure that has to keep working through demand spikes, partial network outages, and third-party API failures outside anyone's control.

Healthcare technology

Systems handling sensitive data under strict access and retention requirements, where the security review is not a formality.

Public sector infrastructure

Long procurement cycles and long system lifespans. We build for maintainability by a team that may not be us in five years.

We evaluate fit case by case. Tell us about the system and we'll give you a direct answer.
