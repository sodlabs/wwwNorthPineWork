What we do

Three disciplines. One team, start to finish.

No handoff between departments. The person who scopes your project is still the one on call for it a year later.

Infrastructure and reliability

Architecture, deployment pipelines, observability. We build for the system's worst day, not its demo day.

Security review

Focused on the paths an attacker would actually take. No 200-item checklist billed by the hour.

Backend engineering

APIs, data models and services sized for the load you actually have, not the load a template assumes.

Process and operations

Incident response, on-call rotation and release practices sized to your actual team, not a template built for 200 engineers.
